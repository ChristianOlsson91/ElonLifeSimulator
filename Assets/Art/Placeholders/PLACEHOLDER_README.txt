PLACEHOLDER ART FOLDER
======================
All sprites, tiles, and character graphics in this folder are TEMPORARY placeholders
for the prototype. They must be replaced with final 16-bit / SNES-inspired pixel art.

Naming convention: prefix files with "placeholder_" (e.g. placeholder_young_elon.png).

Color palette guidance (eras):
- South Africa / Canada early: muted earth tones
- Silicon Valley / modern: more vibrant tech blues and oranges
- Mars: dusty reds and terraforming greens

Do not ship placeholder_ assets as final production art.
